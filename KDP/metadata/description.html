Build embodied AI from first principles, then ship it.

Building Embodied AI is a practitioner's guide to agents that perceive, reason, and act in the physical world, from the mathematics of robotics and control to today's robot foundation models. Across 60 chapters in twelve parts, every core idea is built from scratch and then shown with the modern library that makes it a few lines of code.

What you will learn:

  • Foundations of embodied agents: the agent-environment interface, the perception-action loop, and system architectures
  • The mathematics of robotics and control: coordinate frames and rigid-body transforms, kinematics and dynamics, state estimation and sensor fusion
  • The modern simulation and tooling stack: MuJoCo, MJX, Isaac Lab, and Genesis, plus sim-to-real transfer
  • Reinforcement learning for control: policy gradients, PPO, and reward design
  • Learning from demonstration and robot data: imitation learning, action chunking, and diffusion policies
  • Embodied perception: SLAM, localization, and mapping, and 3D scene representations
  • Language, vision, and action: vision-language models, vision-language-action (VLA) models, and cross-embodiment learning
  • World models and model-based control; robotic manipulation, locomotion, and whole-body humanoid control
  • Multi-agent and human-centered embodiment; evaluation, safety cases, robustness, and deployment
  • Capstone projects and a full course-design guide for instructors

A recurring thread ties it together: the perception-action loop introduced in the first chapter is refined part by part, so classical control, learned policies, and foundation models read as one connected story rather than a catalog of methods. Every concept pairs the underlying theory with runnable code and a "right tool" shortcut that shows the same task in a few lines with a modern library.

With 600+ figures, diagrams, and illustrations, inline math, hands-on labs, and end-of-section exercises, the book takes you from your first coordinate transform to vision-language-action policies and model-based agents.

For engineers, researchers, and graduate students who want both the depth to understand how embodied systems work and the practical skill to build them. Assumes linear algebra, probability, and introductory machine learning; no prior robotics experience required.

Part of the Hands-On AI Science series. Second Edition, fully revised and expanded.
